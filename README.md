| Component | Description | Duration |
|---|---|---|

| Compréhension écrite (CE) | Short texts, signs, messages, adverts | 30 min |

## The Plan

### Phase 1 — Core verbs & present tense
*Weeks 1–3 · ~6 sessions*

**Être & avoir**
- All persons, auxiliary role in compound tenses — everything else builds on these
- Topic 2 · 1 session

**Le présent — regular & irregular**
- Regular -er/-ir/-re patterns, then irregular: aller, venir, faire, pouvoir, vouloir, devoir, tenir, savoir, connaître
- Topics 1, 3–9 · 3 sessions

**Il y a & verbes pronominaux**
- Reflexive verbs (se lever, se coucher etc.) — very common in DELF A2 writing tasks
- Topics 10–11 · 1 session

**Articles — définis, indéfinis, partitifs**
- All three article types with nouns. Common error zone in the writing component.
- Topics 38–40 · 1 session

---

### Phase 2 — Past tenses
*Weeks 4–6 · ~6 sessions*

**Passé composé**
- Avoir vs être auxiliaries, agreement rules, irregular past participles
- Most tested past tense at A2
- Topics 12, 14, 15 · 2 sessions

**Imparfait**
- Formation + when to use vs passé composé — the contrast is a classic exam question
- Topic 16 · 2 sessions

**Former des questions**
- Est-ce que, inversion, intonation — all three forms. Needed for the speaking component.
- Topic 13 · 1 session

**Mots interrogatifs & l'impératif**
- Qui, quoi, où, quand, comment, pourquoi, combien. Imperative for instructions and requests.
- Topics 61, 37 · 1 session

---

### Phase 3 — Future, pronouns & connectors
*Weeks 7–10 · ~8 sessions*

**Le futur simple**
- Regular formation + irregular stems (aller→ir-, être→ser-, avoir→aur- etc.)
- Topics 17–18 · 2 sessions

**Pronouns — COD, COI, y, en, ordre**
- Learn in sequence: subjects → COD → COI → y → en → order
- Heavily tested in reading and writing
- Topics 43–48 · 3 sessions

**Adjectifs possessifs & comparaisons**
- Mon/ma/mes etc., plus/moins/aussi...que comparisons
- Topics 50–52 · 1 session

**Connecteurs & adverbes de temps**
- Parce que, donc, mais, puis, ensuite, enfin — essential for writing a coherent short text
- Topics 60, 69–71 · 2 sessions

---

### Phase 4 — DELF A2 exam skills
*Weeks 11–14 · ongoing*

**Compréhension orale (listening)**
- Resources: RFI Journal en français facile, TV5Monde A2 exercises
- Focus: gist + specific info from everyday dialogues
- 2× per week · 15 min each

**Compréhension écrite (reading)**
- Resources: RFI Savoirs, DELF A2 past papers
- Practice scanning short notices, messages, and adverts
- 2× per week · 15 min each

**Production écrite (writing)**
- Short messages, emails, postcards using grammar from phases 1–3
- Le Petit Prince as a source of model sentences
- 1× per week · 30 min

**Production orale (speaking)**
- Record yourself for 2 min on everyday topics: ma journée, mes projets, mon quartier
- French songs help with intonation passively
- 1× per week · 20 min

### Phase 5 — B1 territory · not now
*After job is sorted*

These topics are on the list but are B1/B2 level. Don't touch them until phases 1–3 are solid.

- **Conditionnel présent & subjonctif** — Topics 22–26
- **Plus-que-parfait, futur antérieur, passé simple** — Topics 19, 21, 33 (note: passé simple appears in L'Étranger but is not tested at A2)
- **Discours indirect, voix passive, concordance des temps** — Topics 34–36
- **Dissertation, nominalisation, thèse-antithèse-synthèse** — Topics 66–68 (B2 academic writing)
- **Pronoms relatifs complexes & composés** — Topics 55–59 (lequel/laquelle, ce dont, ce à quoi)

## Daily 30 Min — How to Use It

**Phases 1–3:** One grammar topic per session.
1. Learn the rule — 5 min
2. Workbook exercises — 15 min
3. Write 3–5 original sentences using it — 10 min

**Phase 4:** Rotate the 4 exam components across the week. Don't try to do all four in one sitting.

**Minimum viable day:** Open the workbook, do one exercise. That's enough to keep the streak alive.
